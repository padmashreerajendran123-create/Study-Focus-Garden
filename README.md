# 🌱 Study Focus Garden

Study Focus Garden is a Flutter-based study productivity application designed to help students stay focused, manage their study sessions, set daily goals, and track their progress.

The app combines productivity features with a virtual garden concept, where users can grow their study garden by completing focused study sessions and achieving their daily goals.

---

## 📱 About the Project

Students often find it difficult to maintain a regular study routine and track their progress.

Study Focus Garden provides a simple and interactive way to:

- ⏱️ Track study sessions
- 🎯 Set and complete daily study goals
- 📊 Monitor study progress
- 🌱 Grow a virtual study garden
- 📅 Keep track of study activities
- 🔥 Build consistent study habits

The main idea is to make studying more engaging by connecting productivity with a virtual garden.

---

## ✨ Features

### ⏱️ Focus Sessions
Users can start focused study sessions and track the amount of time spent studying.

### 🎯 Daily Goals
Users can set daily study goals and work towards completing them.

### 📊 Progress Tracking
The application helps users monitor their study progress and understand their study habits.

### 🌱 Virtual Study Garden
Completed study sessions contribute to the growth of a virtual garden, making the study experience more interactive and motivating.

### 📅 Study Activity Tracking
Users can keep track of their study activities and maintain consistency.
