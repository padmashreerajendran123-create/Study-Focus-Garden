# 🌱 Study Focus Garden

A Flutter study productivity app designed to help students stay focused,
track their study activities, and monitor their progress while growing
a virtual study garden.

## 📱 About the Project

Study Focus Garden combines study tracking with a simple virtual garden
concept. Students can record their study activities, complete focus
sessions, track their daily goals, and see their progress.

## ✨ Features

- 🌱 Study activity tracking
- ⏱️ Focus timer
- 🎯 Daily study goals
- 📊 Study progress tracking
- 🌳 Virtual garden growth
- 📚 Study session tracking
- ✅ Activity completion
- 🏆 Achievement/progress tracking

## 🛠️ Built With

- Flutter
- Dart
